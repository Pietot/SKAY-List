Submission Requirements

Extreme Demon levels must be accepted either by the AREDL or Pointercrate.

OR

The video must be a full run of the level respecting the requirements.

The video must contains the clicks, the cheat indicator enabled and be at least 720p 30fps.

The video must also be unedited and uncut.

The video must be send in the geometry dash channel of the SKAY Discord server and be validated by the owner/admins to be accepted.
